2

Only the facts are saved

We keep your skills, job titles, years of experience and your review, so they are there when you come back.

3

AI reads it, and can be wrong

To review your CV, its text is sent to Google's Gemini AI, and on Google's free tier it may be used to improve Google's products. Scores and matches are guidance, so check them before relying on them.

Questions, answered

Which file types can I upload?

PDF, DOCX, DOC and JPG, up to 5 MB. A text-based PDF or a Word file gives the most accurate result. For a photo, make sure the text is sharp and well lit.

How is the CV score worked out?

An AI model reads your CV and rates eight areas as Good, Can improve or Missing. The score is the average of those ratings. It is a helpful review, not a score from any real employer's system, so treat it as guidance.

Does it work for jobs outside tech?

Yes. Matching uses the titles, skills and experience written in your own CV, whatever the field.

Why only jobs from the last 7 days?

Fresh listings are more likely to still be open. Anything older is filtered out, both in the search and again on our side.

Which countries and cities can I search?

Type any country and, if you like, a city. Some places have fewer listings than others, so a bigger city or a wider search can return more results.

How do I apply?

Every match has an Apply button that opens the original listing, where you apply directly with the employer or job board.

Is this an ATS score?

No. No tool can give a true ATS score, because every company's system works differently. Our score shows how clear, complete and well written your CV is, which helps with both software and people.

What does Tailor do?

It compares the facts in your CV with a job description. You see keywords you already have, keywords the job asks for that your CV does not mention, a suggested headline and stronger action verbs. Only add a skill if it is true for you.

Do you write my CV or cover letter for me?

We review your CV and suggest changes. The cover letter is a first draft with [placeholders] where we do not know a detail. Check it, edit it and make it yours before you send it.

Where are my saved jobs kept?

In your browser, on the device you used to save them. Clearing your browser data removes them.
